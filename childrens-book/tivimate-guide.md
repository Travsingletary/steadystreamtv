# Tommy's TiviMate Adventure
## A SteadyStream Guide for Kids

---

## Page 1: Meet Tommy and SteadyStream
Tommy loves watching his favorite shows on **SteadyStream TV**! Today, his parents are teaching him how to use the TiviMate remote control all by himself.

"TiviMate makes it easy to watch all our favorite channels," says Mom. "Let me show you how!"

---

## Page 2: The Magic Remote
Tommy looks at the remote control. It has lots of buttons, but Mom says he only needs to know a few special ones!

"See these arrow buttons?" Mom points. "These are like magic arrows that help you move around the screen!"

**The Important Buttons:**
- ⬆️ UP Arrow - Move up
- ⬇️ DOWN Arrow - Move down  
- ⬅️ LEFT Arrow - Move left
- ➡️ RIGHT Arrow - Move right
- ⭕ OK/SELECT Button - Pick what you want!

---

## Page 3: Starting TiviMate
Tommy turns on the TV and sees the SteadyStream logo with its beautiful gold color!

"First, use the arrows to find the TiviMate app," says Dad. "Then press the big OK button in the middle of the arrows!"

Tommy presses OK and TiviMate opens up! He sees lots of channels ready to watch.

---

## Page 4: Finding Your Favorite Channels
"Wow! Look at all these channels!" says Tommy.

The screen shows a list of channels:
- Cartoon Network 📺
- Sports Channel ⚽
- Movie Channel 🎬
- Kids Fun Time 🎨

"Use the UP and DOWN arrows to move through the channels," explains Mom. "When you find one you like, press OK!"

---

## Page 5: The Channel List
Tommy uses the DOWN arrow button. The yellow highlight moves down the list!

**How to Navigate:**
1. Press DOWN ⬇️ to see more channels below
2. Press UP ⬆️ to go back to channels above
3. Press OK ⭕ when you find what you want to watch!

"It's like a treasure hunt!" giggles Tommy.

---

## Page 6: Watching Your Show
Tommy finds "Kids Fun Time" and presses OK. The show starts playing!

"If you want to see what else is playing, press the BACK button," says Dad.

**Helpful Buttons:**
- BACK Button ⬅️ - Go back to the channel list
- OK Button ⭕ - Select and play
- HOME Button 🏠 - Return to the main menu

---

## Page 7: The Program Guide (EPG)
"Want to see what's playing later?" asks Mom. She shows Tommy a special button.

"Press this GUIDE button to see the TV schedule!"

Tommy presses it and sees a colorful grid showing:
- What's playing NOW
- What's coming NEXT  
- What's playing TONIGHT

"Use the arrow buttons to look at different shows and times!" 

---

## Page 8: Fast Forward and Rewind (On Recordings)
Dad shows Tommy two more special buttons when watching recorded shows:

"These are like time machines for TV!" 

**Time Control Buttons:**
- ⏩ Fast Forward - Skip ahead quickly
- ⏪ Rewind - Go back to watch again
- ⏸️ Pause - Stop and wait
- ⏯️ Play - Start watching again

"But remember, these only work on recorded shows, not live TV!"

---

## Page 9: The Search Feature
"What if I want to find cartoons?" asks Tommy.

Mom shows him the SEARCH button. "Press this and you can type what you're looking for!"

Tommy sees a keyboard on the screen. Using the arrow buttons, he spells out "C-A-R-T-O-O-N-S."

"Press OK on each letter, then press OK on the search button!" 

All the cartoon channels appear! Magic!

---

## Page 10: The Favorites Star ⭐
"You can save your favorite channels," says Dad. "See this STAR button?"

When Tommy is on his favorite channel, he presses and holds the STAR button. A gold star appears next to the channel name!

"Now your favorite channels are marked with the SteadyStream gold star! You can find them quickly in the Favorites section."

---

## Page 11: Volume Control
"These buttons make the sound louder or quieter," Mom shows Tommy.

**Volume Buttons:**
- 🔊 VOLUME UP - Make it louder
- 🔉 VOLUME DOWN - Make it quieter  
- 🔇 MUTE - Turn off all sound (useful when the phone rings!)

"Always check with a grown-up about how loud the TV should be," reminds Mom.

---

## Page 12: The Settings Menu (With Help)
"There's a special SETTINGS button," says Dad. "But you should ask a grown-up before pressing this one!"

The settings have important things like:
- Picture quality 📺
- Language options 🗣️
- Parental controls 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
- SteadyStream account information

"These help make sure you only watch shows that are right for you!"

---

## Page 13: Turning Everything Off
"When you're done watching TV, it's important to turn everything off properly," says Mom.

**Steps to Power Down:**
1. Press the BACK button until you see the main menu
2. Press the POWER button on the remote
3. Say "Thank you, SteadyStream!" (optional but fun!)

"Always tell a grown-up when you're done watching TV," reminds Dad.

---

## Page 14: Tommy Becomes a TiviMate Expert!
Now Tommy can use TiviMate all by himself! He knows:

✅ How to move around with arrow buttons  
✅ How to select channels with OK  
✅ How to use the program guide  
✅ How to mark favorite channels with stars  
✅ How to search for shows  
✅ When to ask for grown-up help  

"I'm a SteadyStream expert now!" says Tommy proudly.

---

## Page 15: Safety Reminders
**Tommy's Important Rules:**

1. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 **Always ask permission** before watching TV
2. ⏰ **Set a timer** - Don't watch for too long!
3. 🛡️ **Ask a grown-up** if you see something confusing
4. 🔊 **Keep the volume** at a good level
5. 💺 **Sit far enough** from the screen
6. ⚙️ **Never change settings** without asking first

"Following these rules helps keep TV time fun and safe!" says Mom.

---

## Page 16: The End - Happy Streaming!
Tommy gives his parents a big hug!

"Thank you for teaching me how to use TiviMate and SteadyStream TV!" 

Now Tommy can watch his favorite shows anytime. He's careful, he's responsible, and he always follows the rules.

**THE END**

*Enjoy watching with SteadyStream TV - Where Every Show is an Adventure!* ⭐📺✨

## Parent's Guide - Quick Reference

### Essential TiviMate Controls:
- **Arrow Keys (↑ ↓ ← →)**: Navigate menus and channels
- **OK/Select**: Confirm selection
- **Back**: Return to previous screen
- **Home**: Return to main menu
- **Guide/EPG**: View program schedule
- **Search**: Find specific content
- **Favorites (Star ⭐)**: Mark preferred channels
- **Volume (+/-)**: Audio control
- **Mute**: Silence audio
- **Settings**: Configure app (adult supervision recommended)

### Safety Tips:
- Enable parental controls in TiviMate settings
- Set viewing time limits
- Review channel access permissions
- Monitor viewing history
- Establish clear family TV rules
